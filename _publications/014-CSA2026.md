To address the collaborative dispatching decision problem caused by the mutual coupling of local priority, hierarchical resource allocation, and dispatch execution in cross-regional poultry chick dispatching, this study proposes a stage-wise joint optimization and high-level policy enhancement method. First, the three-stage joint optimization (TSJO) algorithm is developed to sequentially complete local absorption, remaining supply-demand allocation, and transportation plan implementation through local matching, cross-regional coordination, and dispatch execution. To address the challenge of difficult fixed parameter adaptation under varying supply-demand structures and execution pressure across different time windows, this study further introduces the proximal policy optimization (PPO) algorithm to perform window-level adaptive selection among predefined strategy templates, forming PPO-TSJO. Experimental results show that TSJO outperforms the global greedy dispatch (GGD), global linear programming dispatch (GLPD), and layered greedy dispatch (LGD) algorithms in terms of the unmet quantity, unmet rate, total dispatch transportation cost, and transportation cost per demand. Compared with TSJO, PPO-TSJO further reduces the unmet quantity and unmet rate, keeps transportation cost basically stable with slight reductions, and maintains generally stable runtime. The results verify the effectiveness of the proposed method in demand satisfaction, cost control, and dispatch execution.
