This work aims to explore the design methodology of game decision-making intelligent agents based on large language models and validate their effectiveness in relevant scenarios. The proposed method utilizes large language models in game decision-making, and constructs agents through techniques such as state text representation, action text representation, expert knowledge injection and supervised fine-tuning, and then validating it through game scenario simulations. The results demonstrate that the agent structure exhibits good adaptability and performance across different scenarios. It indicates that the decision-making agents based on this structure can effectively process complex decision-making problems and provide feasible solutions for practical applications.
